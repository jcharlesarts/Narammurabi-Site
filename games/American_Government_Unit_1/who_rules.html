Concept map

Classify by the real decision-maker.

Start with the person or group that can make the final political decision, not the country name or a symbol on its flag.

  • One: absolute monarchy or dictatorship
  • Few: oligarchy
  • Many: democracy

Compare

Two routes for citizen voice.

Direct democracyCitizens vote on laws or major decisions themselves.
Representative democracyCitizens elect representatives. A republic can also be democratic.

Reality check

Names can hide power.

A constitution, election, or “republic” label is not enough evidence on its own.

  • Can opponents compete safely?
  • Can citizens remove leaders?
  • Can courts and media check power?
